Legenda: support

Last updated 22 November 2026.

Your iPhone writes down what is said around it, while it is said. A translation appears below when you want one. Speech recognition and translation run on the phone.

1 Before you startRequirements

Device
iPhone with iOS 26 or later
Download
A network connection and space for about 630 MB of speech model files, plus about 2 MB for voice detection. The prepared model and translation language files need more space.
Preparation
A few minutes to prepare the speech model on first start
Tested hardware
Tested so far only on Apple's latest A-series chip, the A20 Pro

2 SetupFirst start

  1. Open Legenda with a network connection. The screen says Setting up Legenda on this iPhone. Let Download the speech model finish.
  2. Wait for Prepare it for the Neural Engine. This can take several minutes.
  3. Keep the app open, or tap Continue in background when it appears. If you leave the app, open it again to finish the first test.
  4. Allow microphone access when iOS asks. This happens after model preparation.
  5. Speak near the phone. The app shows Listening and displays captions.

3 Common questionsSetup seems stuck at preparation

Preparation can take several minutes, especially for the encoder. Keep Legenda open to finish setup. The phone can get warm during this work.

Common questionsWhy does the app prepare the model again after an update?

An update can require preparation again. The app may show Optimizing the speech model for this update. Complete downloaded models are reused.

Common questionsWhy do no captions appear?

Allow microphone access for Legenda in iOS Settings. If the app shows Paused, tap the play button to continue. Speak closer to the phone in one of the six supported languages listed below.

Common questionsHow do I translate and change the target language?

Tap Translate to show translation below the captions. Tap the target language name above the translation to choose another target language. iOS downloads a language pair the first time you need it if it is not already installed.

Common questionsHow do I read earlier lines and return to Live?

Scroll up to read earlier captions; new captions keep your reading position. Tap Live or scroll to the bottom to follow new captions again. Translation scrolls independently and has its own Live translation button.

Common questionsHow do I share, copy or clear the transcript?

Open the Share menu and choose Share transcript or Copy transcript. The transcript contains captions, not the translation. Tap the trash button, labelled Clear, then confirm Clear in Clear transcript and translation? to clear both.

Common questionsDoes it work without a network?

Yes, captions work without a network once the speech model and voice detection model are installed. Translation works without a network once the needed language pair is installed. First setup, missing model files and new translation language pairs need a network connection.

Common questionsWhich languages are supported?

The six supported languages are English, French, German, Italian, Portuguese and Spanish. The app detects the spoken language for each finalized caption. The same six languages are available as the target language.

Common questionsWhy does the phone get warm or use battery?

Speech recognition runs on the phone while it listens, so the phone can get warm and use battery. The app adjusts recognition frequency when iOS reports high heat. Tap Pause when you do not need captions.

Common questionsHow do I delete everything?

Delete the app to remove its downloaded models and local settings. There is no account to delete. Copies of the transcript or diagnostic log that you saved elsewhere remain there.
