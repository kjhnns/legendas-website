Legendas: privacy policy

Last updated 24 November 2026. Applies to the Legendas iPhone app.

Legendas does not upload your audio or your captions by itself. You can share or copy the transcript, or export a diagnostic log that contains caption text. The developer runs no server for this app. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Speech recognition and translation run on your iPhone. Section 4 describes the network requests.

1 AboutWho runs this app

Legendas is a personal project of Johannes Klumpe, Zurich, Switzerland.

2 AudioMicrophone

The app uses the microphone only while it listens. Audio is processed on the phone and is not sent anywhere.

Listening continues when you leave the app or lock the screen. The microphone stays on, with the iOS recording indicator. The app transcribes in the background when iOS permits it. If iOS refuses access to the Neural Engine, audio stays on the phone. The app tries to transcribe it when you return. The audio buffer holds up to 30 seconds in memory plus up to 512 MiB in unlinked temporary files. Each file's name is removed before audio is written. The files are deleted as their audio is consumed or when the app process closes. Pause stops the microphone immediately. The app then tries to finish the buffered audio. If processing is blocked or fails, the audio stays until processing succeeds or the app process closes. You can also delete the buffered audio by confirming Clear. Background listening is in testing and comes with a later update. The version 1.0.0 build submitted for review does not include it.

3 TextCaptions and transcript

Captions and the transcript exist in the app's memory. The app does not save a session. The live transcript is lost when the app process closes.

Text leaves the app only when you act: Share transcript, Copy transcript, or Export log (includes captions) in the hidden diagnostics panel. The diagnostic log includes caption text, timing, and model and cache diagnostics with local file paths. Exporting it writes a file to the app's temporary storage for the share sheet. That file can remain there afterwards. Clear removes the transcript and the translation. It does not erase diagnostic-log entries or an exported file.

The app stores downloaded models, preparation information and your settings on the phone.

4 DownloadsNetwork requests

  1. Setup. On first start, the app downloads the speech model (about 630 MB) and the voice detection model (about 2 MB). The open-source FluidAudio library performs these downloads from Hugging Face (huggingface.co). The app's own code also requests the file list of the speech model from Hugging Face. As with any download, the download provider receives your IP address and standard request data. Its privacy policy applies to these requests. The app's own request and the app's calls to the downloader carry no account, no token and no identifier of the app's own.
  2. Translation. Apple's Translation framework runs on the device. If a language pair is not installed, iOS downloads it from Apple the first time you need it. That download is done by iOS under Apple's privacy policy.
  3. After setup. The app's own code makes no other network request. With the speech model, voice detection model and needed translation language pairs installed, captions and translation work without a network. Missing model files or new translation language pairs need a download.

5 AppleSystem reports

If you agreed to share analytics with app developers in the iOS settings, Apple provides the developer with crash reports and aggregated usage statistics. If you use a TestFlight build, Apple provides the developer with crash reports and the feedback you choose to send. The app adds no analytics or crash-reporting service.

6 AccessPermissions

The app requests microphone permission only. It does not use the camera, contacts, photos, calendar, location or health data.

7 ConversationsOther people

The app transcribes what the microphone hears. Tell the people present. You are responsible for following the rules on recording and transcribing conversations where you are.

8 AudienceChildren

The app is not directed at children.
